Quick answers

Frequently asked

How fast do you reply to messages?

Most messages are answered within 5 minutes during support hours (Mon–Sat, 9am–7pm IST). Outside those hours the helpline is still staffed 24/7 for SOS and breakdowns.

How do I report damage or an accident?

Open the app, tap Help → Report incident and add photos, location and a short description within 24 hours. A claims officer responds within 2 business hours.

When does my safety deposit get released?

A clean return releases the hold within 3–7 business days, depending on your bank. Damage or fines may deduct first, and you'll see an itemised breakdown in the app.

Can I cancel a booking and get a refund?

Free cancellation up to 2 hours before pickup — full refund, no questions. After that, write to support@cityride.in and we'll sort it within a day.

Ready to power a fleet for your business?

We love partnering with delivery services, hotels and local businesses. Choose "Business partnership" in the form and our fleet team will reach out within 1 business day.
