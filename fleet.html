Good to know

Fleet questions, answered

Do I need a driving licence?

Yes — a valid two-wheeler licence is mandatory and verified during signup.

Can I swap the vehicle mid-booking?

Yes, free of charge — just call support and park-swap at any zone.

Are electric models charged on pickup?

Always. E-scooters leave the station at 100% and battery stats are shown in-app.

What is the minimum age?

18 years with a valid licence. Under-21 riders get the 50cc mopeds only.
