Our story

From five mopeds to a citywide fleet

CityRide began in 2019 as a two-person operation in a rented garage. We repaired a handful of second-hand mopeds, slapped on our teal paint, and put them on the street with hand-painted unlock codes.

Today we run a connected fleet of 180+ scooters and electric mopeds across 12 zones, but every decision is still measured against our founding rule: keep it simple, keep it safe, keep it affordable.

See Our Fleet

Our Mission

To remove every barrier between people and the freedom of a two-wheeler — using pricing that is transparent, and a network that is always within walking distance.

Our Vision

A city where nobody owns a second vehicle that sits idle. By 2030, we want one in ten urban trips to happen on a shared, electric ride from CityRide.

Milestones

Our journey so far

  1. 2019 · The garage

    First five mopeds hit the street

    Hand-painted unlock codes and a helpline that was just a personal phone.

  2. 2021 · Going digital

    App, unlock codes and live tracking

    Fleet grew to 60 vehicles and we launched GPS-based self-service parking.

  3. 2023 · Electric first

    E-scooter line joins the fleet

    40% of our fleet now runs on batteries with free swap stations in every zone.

  4. 2026 · Today

    180+ vehicles · 12 zones · 24k riders

    Opening our first electric-only neighbourhood hub later this year.
