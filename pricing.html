FAQ

Pricing questions

When do I pay?

Hold the booking free. Pay at unlock — the clock starts when the scooter starts.

What happens if I return late?

A 15-minute grace period, then the next hourly slot is charged. Weekly and monthly plans pause instead.

Are fuel costs included?

Yes. Petrol models leave full and you never top up. Electric models are always charged to 100%.

Can I upgrade mid-plan?

Anytime. You only pay the difference for the remaining days of your current plan.

Ready to ride at these rates?

Lock today's prices — they won't change for your whole plan.
