Insurance included

Every rental automatically includes comprehensive cover up to ₹2 lakh for the scooter and third-party liability up to the legal limit as per the Motor Vehicles Act.

  • ✓ In-scooter damage & theft cover
  • ✓ Third-party damage & liability
  • ✓ Personal accident cover up to ₹5 lakh for the rider
  • ✓ No medical documents needed

Claim process

  1. 1. Report any incident in-app within 24 hours.
  2. 2. Add photos, location and a brief description.
  3. 3. A claims officer responds within 2 business hours.
  4. 4. Settled claims are paid within 7 working days.

Always report incidents before leaving the scene. Unreported or single-vehicle incidents may affect liability cover.

Liability

Who pays for what

Covered by us

  • → Normal wear & tear
  • → First minor scratch / scuff
  • → Mechanical breakdowns
  • → Battery exhaustion (electric)
  • → Tyre punctures away from station

Paid by you

  • → Damage from rash or unauthorised use
  • → Loss or theft of helmets & accessories
  • → Traffic fines & challans
  • → Damage while riding under influence

Excess / deductibles

For claimable damage, a fixed excess applies per category — Economy ₹1,000, Commuter ₹1,500, Premium ₹2,500, Sports ₹3,500. The zero-liability add-on removes this excess entirely.

Ground rules

Rental rules

Age & licence

🪪

Minimum 18 years with a valid two-wheeler licence, verified at signup and spot-checked at pickup.

Helmets required

🪖

Riders and pillions must wear the provided helmets. No helmet, no ride — no exceptions.

Single rider sharing

👥

Only the booked rider may drive. The registered second rider is the only exception.

Stay in zones

📍

End every trip inside a marked CityRide bay. Out-of-zone parking is towed at the rider's cost.

No alcohol or drugs

🚫

Zero tolerance. Riding under the influence voids cover and deposits entirely.

Damage within 20 min

🛠️

Report pre-existing damage within 20 minutes of unlock to avoid any dispute.

Good to know

Safe riding tips

  • ★ Do a 60-second walk-around before every trip — mirrors, brakes, tyres.
  • ★ Keep 2 seconds of follow distance, double it in rain.
  • ★ Use the phone mount — never hold the phone while riding.
  • ★ Prefer ride lanes and avoid footpaths; pedestrians always have right of way.
  • ★ If the low-battery light glows and you're far from a station, call the helpline for a swap.

Emergency? Dial 24/7 helpline +91 98765 43210 or use the SOS button in the app.
