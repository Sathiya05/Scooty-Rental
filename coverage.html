Coverage FAQ

Common questions

Can I return in a different zone?

Yes — one-way rides are free between any two stations within the city limits.

What if a station is full?

Bring the scooter to the nearest CityRide bay and get ₹50 ride credit for the detour.

Do you cover the suburbs?

Not yet — new zones are added every quarter. Nominate yours and win free rides.

Is parking free?

Always, inside our marked bays. Parking outside a zone incurs a small relocation fee.
